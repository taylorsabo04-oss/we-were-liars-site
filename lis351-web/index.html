Features

Author

The author of the book is E. Lockhart. She wrote the book in May of 2014.

Director

The director of the TV show adaptation is Julie Plec. She worked alongside Amazon MGM Studios.

Cast

Cadence, the main character, is played by Emily Alyn Lind. The supporting cast is played by Joseph Zada, Shubham Maheshwari, and Esther McGregor.

Featured Content

The story follows Cadence Sinclair, a girl with a summer's worth of lost memories, as she tries to piece together the past. She does so on her family's island, alongside her two cousins and love interest.
